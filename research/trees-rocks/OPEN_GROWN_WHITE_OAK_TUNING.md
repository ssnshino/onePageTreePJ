# OPEN-GROWN WHITE OAK — TUNING NOTE

Created: 2026-09-24T21:15:00+09:00

## Source-derived morphology

US Forest Service describes open-grown White Oak as having a short, stocky bole and a widespreading rugged crown.

Morton Arboretum describes White Oak as massive and long-lived, with wide-spreading horizontal branches, and lists mature width around 100 ft versus mature height 50–80 ft.

NC State describes mature White Oak as broad, rounded, spreading and dense.

Sources:
- https://www.srs.fs.usda.gov/pubs/misc/ag_654/volume_2/quercus/alba.htm
- https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/white-oak/
- https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/quercus-alba/
